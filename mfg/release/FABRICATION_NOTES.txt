NOTES
Panel: 245.2 x 223.2 mm, 4 layers, 1.6 mm; JLCPCB stack-up JLC041611-7628 (1 oz outer and inner copper, 7628 prepreg each side of a 1.065 mm core).  Purple mask, white silkscreen, ENIG.
Six designs on one customer panel: base, tree (turned 90 degrees), perch, bat, owl token, cat token, joined by tabs with 132 mouse-bite holes; 3 fiducials (a 1 mm dot in a 2 mm mask opening, a pad on each side) and 4 tooling holes on the 5 mm rails.
Minimum track/space on the board: 0.150 mm.  Vias 0.45/0.25 mm, 1059 of them; smallest drill 0.25 mm.
The gold moon and ghost (the theremin's antennas) are bare ENIG copper under mask openings: not a mistake.
The copper coils (L3-L7, L201, L301, L401, L501) are spirals drawn in the footprints, not parts: nothing to fit.
Through-hole parts JLCPCB fits, all on the TOP side: J1 (barrel jack), J3 (2x8 socket), LS1 (speaker).
Hand solder (hand_solder_bom.csv), after the boards are snapped apart: J101, J102, J201 -- J101: right-angle header off the tree's foot: its pins would hang over the panel's rail; J102: right-angle header off the tree's top: its pins would hang over the perch; J201: through-hole socket on the perch's underside: keeps the line's through-hole work on one side.
Upload: OWLternatingCurrent_bom.csv and OWLternatingCurrent_cpl_jlcpcb_upload.csv (JLCPCB's conventions: the bottom-side mirror, rotations read in previews or predicted from their drawings, parts moved to their library's origin); OWLternatingCurrent_cpl_kicad_native.csv is KiCad's own numbers for reference.
THEN READ THE PLACEMENT PREVIEW against PLACEMENT_CHECKS.txt, both sides: the predicted rotations, the polarity marks of the diodes and LEDs, and the parts moved to JLCPCB's datum (SW3 needed a further nudge north on 2026-10-09: mfg/jlc_rotation.py JLC_DATUM_READINGS).
Coordinates: the panel's top-left corner is the drill/place origin; the gerbers, the drill file and the CPL all use that frame (x right, y up in the CPL), so they agree.
Shipping: UPS Express Saver (DDP) or DHL Express Priority (DDP): a US individual must ship DDP, and JLCPCB collects the duty (about 35% of the PCBs and assembly).  Lead time: 4-layer PCBs 2-5 days, Standard assembly at least 4 more (+1 for through-hole parts), then 4-5 business days in the post.
Checks passed: DRC 0 violations, 0 unconnected, 0 parity; 146 filled zone polygons; every netlist part on the board; every code matched (gen/parts.py's words, the value, the package, the MPN) and in stock; no ordered part wants a hole; the CPL agrees with KiCad's position export, and JLCPCB's drawings placed by it land on our pads (30 chips, both sides).
